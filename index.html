Region fitting compares robust local foreground and surrounding-background statistics.

This browser prototype uses voxel-sampled coordinate descent, not RCTL’s production Kalman-filter / Doo–Sabin pipeline. “Smooth” fits an ellipsoid, “rigid” a fixed primitive, and “CAD-like” a primitive with one global scale parameter. No mesh rendering is implemented.
